# v4 — ignoring the clutter clears the port strip

[V4_CHECK.md](V4_CHECK.md) scored rule B on every known dry pixel and stopped. Kop van Zuid passed. The port strip did not: layover minus nominal was +1.18 / +1.04 dB in the near and middle thirds, and the bar was +1.5. The same labels, scored only away from clutter, had been +1.94 / +3.64. The shortfall was the port. Container stacks, cranes, ships and trees are not in a buildings-only label, so their bright returns sat in the nominal class and pulled the contrast down.

This note does not move the roof. Rule B (`image_space.own_range_grid`), the buildings-only scene, and the AHN4 DTM ground stand. There is no roof-nominal edit. What changes is where a label is claimed. Mask value 255 means the pixel is ignored: it is out of the loss, the class weights, every score, and the brightness baseline. v2 was not modified. The v2 image, incidence, look, grid, split and orientation are copied. `label_version` is `v4-inplace-dtm-ign`.

## Which strips are in the set

A strip is in when, on its v2 tiles, layover minus nominal is at least +1.0 dB in both the near third and the middle third. Water and ignored pixels are out of that contrast. Below +1.5 dB is flagged, not dropped. A tile is dropped only when more than 60% of it is ignored.

Eighteen v2 strips. Fifteen included, three left out. 3042 tiles kept: 2918 train, 95 val, 29 test. 461 tiles on the included strips were dropped for the 60% rule. The three left-out strips account for the other 466 v2 tiles. 3969 − 461 − 466 = 3042.

## What the kept tiles contain

Classes are `nominal`, `layover`, `shadow`. Shares below are of non-ignored pixels.

| split | tiles | nominal | layover | shadow | pixels |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| train | 2918 | 0.940 | 0.051 | 0.0095 | 5.59×10⁸ |
| val | 95 | 0.668 | 0.280 | 0.052 | 1.70×10⁷ |
| test | 29 | 0.568 | 0.364 | 0.068 | 4.77×10⁶ |

The split is the v2 west-to-east blocks, unchanged. Val and test are small and much richer in layover than train. A mean IoU on val is not a mean IoU on a random training tile.

Coverage (surfaces per pixel, the severity target) has median 1.0 on nearly every strip. The 90th percentile is 1 or 3. Overlapping layover is a thin tail, not the typical pixel. Ignored pixels keep their coverage value; they are skipped through the mask.

`norm_stats.json` is copied from v2. Same images, same train split.

On a random sample of train tiles the trainer saw 26.5% of pixels ignored, and class frequencies of the rest 0.933 / 0.056 / 0.011. That matches the train row above.

## Training

`train.py` reads `classes.json`. On this set it prints 3 classes and the ignore fraction. It was not in the repo before this work, and this commit does not add it.

The overfit at batch 4 does not fit in 4 GB. The kernel killed it (exit 137) before a step was written, and killed it again with `--workers 0`. One overfit of a single batch then ran 200 steps on CPU:

| step | loss | nominal | layover | shadow |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 0.915 | 0.82 | 0.00 | 0.00 |
| 199 | 0.028 | 1.00 | 0.82 | 0.00 |

The loss collapsed and layover rose to 0.82. Shadow printing 0.00 is not a result about that class. The score divides by `max(tp+fp+fn, 1)`, so a class with no pixels in the batch also prints 0.00.

There is no `runs/v4/metrics.json`. The full run (`--out runs/v4 --batch 8`) was not started: batch 4 already does not fit here, and a 50-epoch pass over 2918 tiles does not fit a session on this CPU. There is no `runs/v2_base/metrics.json` in this workspace to put next to it. That comparison needs a GPU.
